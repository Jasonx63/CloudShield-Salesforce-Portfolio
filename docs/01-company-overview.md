# Company Overview

## Objective

Establish the fictional company that will be used throughout this Salesforce portfolio and define the business environment in which the sales process takes place.

---

## Company Profile

**Company Name:** CloudShield Security

**Industry:** Cybersecurity Software (SaaS)

**Business Model:** Business-to-Business (B2B)

**Headquarters:** Nampa, Idaho

CloudShield Security provides cloud-based cybersecurity solutions that help organizations protect endpoints, monitor threats, and improve their overall security posture through subscription-based software and managed security services.

---

## Target Customers

CloudShield Security serves organizations that require enterprise-grade cybersecurity solutions, including:

- Healthcare
- Government
- Education
- Financial Services
- Commercial Businesses

---

## Primary Product Offering

The company's flagship platform is **Haze**, a cloud-based endpoint security solution available in multiple editions to support organizations of different sizes and security requirements.

Additional services include:

- Premium Support
- Managed Detection & Response (MDR)
- Security Awareness Training

---

## Business Goals

CloudShield Security focuses on:

- Acquiring new customers
- Expanding existing customer accounts
- Increasing Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
- Maintaining high customer renewal rates
- Delivering long-term customer success
