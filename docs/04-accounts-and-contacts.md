# Accounts and Contacts

## Overview

CloudShield Security manages customer relationships using Salesforce Accounts and Contacts to represent organizations and their key stakeholders. Each account includes realistic business information, primary decision makers, procurement contacts, and supporting opportunity records to simulate a real-world B2B SaaS sales environment.
