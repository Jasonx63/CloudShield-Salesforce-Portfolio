# Opportunities

## Overview

CloudShield Security manages its sales pipeline through Salesforce Opportunities, tracking each customer from initial qualification through closed business. Every opportunity in this project is tied to an Account, Contacts, Products, Price Books, Contact Roles, Activities, and Quotes to demonstrate a complete B2B SaaS sales workflow.
