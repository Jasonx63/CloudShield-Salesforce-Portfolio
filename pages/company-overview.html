Company Overview

An overview of CloudShield Security’s business model, cybersecurity services, customers, mission, and role within this Salesforce Sales Cloud implementation.

Company Profile

CloudShield Security is a fictional cybersecurity software company created to demonstrate a realistic Salesforce Sales Cloud implementation.

The company provides cloud-based cybersecurity products and managed security services to commercial, healthcare, education, manufacturing, and government organizations.

Business Model

CloudShield operates primarily through recurring SaaS subscriptions. Customers may purchase annual or multi-year agreements based on company size, security requirements, support needs, and industry.

The company also offers optional managed security services and premium support packages that can be included within customer opportunities and quotes.

Mission

To help organizations protect their systems, users, and information through accessible, reliable, and scalable cybersecurity solutions.

Vision

To become a trusted cybersecurity partner for organizations that need enterprise-level protection without unnecessary complexity.

Core Values

Customer Trust

Build lasting relationships through transparency, reliability, and responsible service.

Security First

Place customer protection and risk reduction at the center of every product and service.

Simplicity

Make cybersecurity solutions easier for customers to understand, purchase, and use.

Continuous Improvement

Improve products, sales processes, and customer outcomes through data and feedback.

Target Customers

The Salesforce implementation includes customer scenarios across four major industries:

  • Healthcare
  • Education
  • Government
  • Manufacturing

Product and Service Portfolio

  • Haze Essentials
  • Haze Professional
  • Haze Enterprise
  • Premium Support
  • Managed Detection and Response

Salesforce Purpose

Salesforce Sales Cloud serves as the central platform for managing customer records, contacts, leads, opportunities, products, price books, quotes, activities, reports, dashboards, and renewals.

The implementation demonstrates how connected CRM records support a complete B2B SaaS sales lifecycle rather than functioning as isolated Salesforce exercises.
