Opportunities

An overview of how CloudShield Security uses Salesforce Opportunities to manage pipeline, customer needs, products, pricing, stakeholders, activities, and projected revenue.

Opportunity Management

Salesforce Opportunities represent active and potential revenue-generating sales engagements.

Each Opportunity connects the customer Account, Contacts, products, price book, activities, expected close date, sales stage, and projected revenue within one organized record.

Opportunity Structure

The CloudShield opportunity records were created to reflect realistic B2B SaaS and cybersecurity sales scenarios.

  • Opportunity name
  • Customer Account
  • Sales stage
  • Expected close date
  • Opportunity amount
  • Products and quantities
  • Assigned price book
  • Customer Contacts
  • Tasks and activities
  • Related quotes

Sales Stages

Opportunities move through a structured sales process as customer requirements become clearer and the likelihood of closing increases.

Prospecting

Initial research, outreach, and identification of a potential customer need.

Qualification

Confirmation of business need, stakeholders, budget, urgency, and product fit.

Needs Analysis

Discovery of security requirements, user counts, service needs, and customer priorities.

Proposal / Price Quote

Presentation of selected products, pricing, quantities, services, and proposed terms.

Negotiation / Review

Review of pricing, contract requirements, implementation considerations, and objections.

Closed

The Opportunity is marked Closed Won or Closed Lost based on the final customer decision.

Products and Revenue

Products are added to Opportunities as line items after the appropriate price book has been selected.

Each line item includes a product, quantity, and sales price. Salesforce uses those values to calculate the total Opportunity amount.

Example Product

Haze Professional

Example Quantity

500 users

Example Unit Price

$120 per user

Calculated Total

$60,000

Contact Roles

Opportunity Contact Roles connect customer stakeholders to a specific sales engagement.

This provides visibility into the primary point of contact, decision-maker, technical evaluator, executive sponsor, and other participants.

Activities and Next Steps

Tasks and activities help the sales team document customer interactions and maintain momentum.

  • Conduct discovery meetings
  • Confirm user counts and product requirements
  • Schedule product demonstrations
  • Engage additional decision-makers
  • Prepare pricing and proposals
  • Follow up on customer feedback
  • Track negotiation and approval steps

Pipeline Reporting

Opportunity data is used to create reports and dashboards that provide visibility into projected revenue and sales performance.

Pipeline Value

Measures the total value of active sales opportunities.

Stage Distribution

Shows where Opportunities are positioned throughout the sales process.

Revenue by Industry

Compares opportunity value across customer segments.

Expected Close Dates

Supports forecasting and prioritization of upcoming deals.

Business Value

Opportunity management gives CloudShield Security a consistent process for qualifying deals, tracking revenue, coordinating customer engagement, and progressing each sale toward a clear outcome.

The connected data also supports forecasting, reporting, quoting, renewals, and future account expansion.
