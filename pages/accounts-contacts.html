Accounts & Contacts

An overview of how CloudShield Security uses Salesforce Accounts and Contacts to organize customer organizations, decision-makers, stakeholders, and sales relationships.

Customer Record Structure

Salesforce Accounts represent the organizations that CloudShield Security works with, while Contacts represent the individual people associated with those organizations.

This structure allows the sales team to maintain a complete view of each customer relationship, including stakeholders, active opportunities, communication history, quotes, and future expansion potential.

Account Management

Each customer organization is maintained as an Account record within Salesforce.

Accounts provide a central location for company information and related sales activity.

  • Organization name
  • Industry
  • Customer type
  • Location
  • Related contacts
  • Open and closed opportunities
  • Activities and follow-up tasks
  • Quotes and sales history

Customer Accounts

The CloudShield Security Salesforce environment includes customer scenarios from several industries.

Contact Management

Contacts represent the individual stakeholders involved in customer evaluations, purchasing decisions, implementation planning, and ongoing account management.

Maintaining accurate Contact records helps the sales team understand each person’s role within the buying process.

  • Primary point of contact
  • Decision-maker
  • Technical evaluator
  • Procurement representative
  • Executive sponsor
  • End-user stakeholder

Primary Contact Example

Sarah serves as the primary point of contact in the Boise State University customer scenario.

Her Contact record is connected to the Account and can also be associated with the related Opportunity and Quote.

Mr. Chen is referenced as an additional decision-maker, demonstrating how multiple customer stakeholders may participate in the same sales process.

Contact Roles

Opportunity Contact Roles help document how each person is involved in a specific sales opportunity.

This is especially useful when an Account contains several Contacts with different responsibilities.

Relationship Visibility

Shows which customer stakeholders are connected to each opportunity.

Decision Process

Helps the sales team identify influencers, evaluators, and final decision-makers.

Activities and Follow-Up

Tasks and activities were created within Salesforce to support consistent customer follow-up.

These activities help document next steps and keep opportunities moving through the sales process.

  • Schedule discovery meetings
  • Confirm customer requirements
  • Coordinate product demonstrations
  • Follow up on proposals
  • Engage decision-makers
  • Prepare renewal and expansion discussions

Business Value

Connecting Accounts, Contacts, Opportunities, Quotes, and Activities provides a complete view of the customer relationship.

Centralized Records

Customer and stakeholder information is stored in one connected CRM system.

Improved Follow-Up

Tasks and activities help ensure that customer commitments and next steps are not missed.

Stronger Relationships

Contact roles provide visibility into the people involved in each buying decision.

Account Growth

Existing customer records support renewals, upselling, cross-selling, and future expansion.
