Price Books

An overview of the Standard and Government price books used to support consistent pricing across CloudShield Security opportunities and customer quotes.

Price Book Overview

Salesforce Price Books organize the prices available for CloudShield Security products and services.

Each price book contains product entries that can be selected when creating opportunity products and quote line items.

This structure helps ensure that sales representatives use approved pricing based on the customer type and sales scenario.

Standard Price Book

The Standard Price Book supports commercial customer opportunities across healthcare, education, manufacturing, and other private-sector industries.

It serves as the primary pricing source for standard CloudShield subscriptions, support packages, and managed services.

Customer Type

Commercial and non-government organizations.

Primary Use

Standard opportunities, proposals, expansions, and renewals.

Government Price Book

The Government Price Book supports public-sector customers and government cybersecurity initiatives.

It allows CloudShield Security to maintain separate pricing for government opportunities while using the same underlying product catalog.

Customer Type

Federal, state, local, and public-sector organizations.

Primary Use

Government proposals, modernization projects, and public-sector agreements.

Product Entries

Products must have an active price book entry before they can be added to an opportunity or quote.

CloudShield products were added to both the Standard and Government price books so they could be used across multiple customer scenarios.

  • Haze Essentials
  • Haze Professional
  • Haze Enterprise
  • Premium Support
  • Managed Detection and Response

Salesforce Workflow

The selected price book determines which product prices are available for an opportunity.

After selecting a price book, products can be added as opportunity line items with a quantity and sales price. Those values contribute to the opportunity amount and can later be included within a customer quote.

  • Select the appropriate customer opportunity
  • Assign the correct price book
  • Add products to the opportunity
  • Enter quantities and approved sales prices
  • Review the calculated opportunity total
  • Create and synchronize the customer quote

Business Value

Separate price books allow CloudShield Security to support multiple pricing strategies without creating duplicate product records.

This structure improves pricing consistency, simplifies proposal creation, and provides greater control over commercial and government sales motions.

Consistency

Approved prices remain connected to the correct Salesforce products.

Flexibility

Different customer segments can use separate pricing strategies.

Accuracy

Opportunity and quote totals are calculated using structured product entries.

Scalability

Additional markets or pricing models can be added through new price books.
