Quotes

An overview of how CloudShield Security uses Salesforce Quotes to organize products, pricing, quantities, customer contacts, proposal totals, and synchronized opportunity data.

Quote Management

Salesforce Quotes provide a structured way to present selected products, services, quantities, and pricing to a customer.

Each Quote is connected to an Opportunity, helping maintain consistency between the active sales deal and the proposal presented to the customer.

Quote Structure

CloudShield Quote records were created to reflect realistic SaaS and cybersecurity proposals.

  • Quote name
  • Related Opportunity
  • Customer Account
  • Primary Contact
  • Expiration date
  • Selected price book
  • Products and services
  • Quantities
  • Sales prices
  • Calculated total
  • Quote synchronization status

Quote Creation Process

Quotes are created after the customer’s needs, product requirements, and pricing structure have been confirmed.

  • Open the related Opportunity
  • Create a new Quote
  • Confirm the customer Account
  • Add the primary Contact
  • Select the appropriate price book
  • Add products and services
  • Enter quantities and sales prices
  • Review the calculated total
  • Synchronize the Quote with the Opportunity

Products and Quote Lines

Products are added to the Quote as line items using the pricing available within the selected price book.

Each line item records the product, quantity, unit price, and calculated total.

Core Subscriptions

Haze Essentials, Haze Professional, and Haze Enterprise.

Optional Services

Premium Support and Managed Detection and Response.

Pricing Source

Standard or Government Price Book.

Calculation

Quantity multiplied by the approved sales price.

Government Proposal Example

A government-focused Quote was created to support a cybersecurity modernization initiative.

The proposal demonstrates how Salesforce can organize public-sector pricing, customer information, selected products, service add-ons, quantities, and total contract value within one record.

Proposal Type

Government cybersecurity modernization

Pricing Model

Government Price Book

Customer Need

Scalable cybersecurity protection and support services.

Salesforce Value

Centralized proposal and opportunity management.

Primary Contact

The customer’s primary Contact can be associated with the Quote to ensure that the proposal is connected to the appropriate stakeholder.

In the Boise State University scenario, Sarah was added as the primary point of contact for the Quote.

Quote Synchronization

Salesforce allows one Quote to be synchronized with its related Opportunity.

When synchronization is active, Quote line items and totals can remain aligned with the Opportunity products and amount.

Data Consistency

Helps keep Opportunity and Quote product data aligned.

Revenue Accuracy

Reduces differences between proposed pricing and pipeline value.

Proposal Review

Before presenting a Quote to a customer, the sales representative should verify all proposal details.

  • Correct customer Account
  • Correct primary Contact
  • Approved price book
  • Accurate product selection
  • Correct user quantities
  • Approved pricing
  • Included support and managed services
  • Accurate proposal total
  • Valid expiration date

Business Value

Salesforce Quotes connect customer requirements, approved pricing, products, services, and Opportunity data within a repeatable proposal workflow.

Consistency

Proposals use structured products and approved price book entries.

Visibility

Sales teams can review proposal status and value from the related Opportunity.

Accuracy

Quantities and prices are calculated through organized Quote line items.

Scalability

The same Quote process can support commercial, government, renewal, and expansion scenarios.
