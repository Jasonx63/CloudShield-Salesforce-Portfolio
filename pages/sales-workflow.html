Sales Workflow

A complete overview of the CloudShield Security Salesforce sales process, from initial lead generation through account management, opportunity development, quoting, reporting, renewals, and expansion.

Workflow Overview

The CloudShield Security Salesforce environment was designed to demonstrate a connected B2B SaaS sales workflow rather than isolated CRM records.

Customer information, stakeholders, opportunities, products, price books, quotes, activities, reports, and dashboards work together throughout the sales lifecycle.

Sales Process

1. Lead Generation

Identify potential customers through research, referrals, inbound interest, campaigns, and outbound prospecting.

2. Lead Qualification

Evaluate customer need, organization fit, urgency, stakeholders, budget, and potential product alignment.

3. Lead Conversion

Convert a qualified Lead into an Account, Contact, and Opportunity while preserving the customer’s information.

4. Discovery

Confirm cybersecurity requirements, user counts, business objectives, technical needs, and decision criteria.

5. Solution Development

Select the appropriate Haze subscription, support options, managed services, and pricing model.

6. Proposal Creation

Build a Salesforce Quote containing approved products, quantities, pricing, contacts, and proposal totals.

7. Negotiation

Address customer questions, revise scope, review pricing, and coordinate approvals.

8. Close

Mark the Opportunity Closed Won or Closed Lost based on the customer’s final decision.

Lead to Customer Conversion

Qualified Leads are converted into connected Salesforce records.

  • The Account represents the customer organization.
  • The Contact represents the individual stakeholder.
  • The Opportunity represents the potential revenue.

This conversion process helps preserve customer data while moving the relationship into the active sales pipeline.

Account and Contact Management

The Account acts as the central customer record. Related Contacts represent the stakeholders involved in discovery, evaluation, procurement, approval, and implementation.

Opportunity Contact Roles provide additional context by identifying how each stakeholder participates in a specific sales engagement.

Opportunity Development

The Opportunity is updated as the sales process progresses.

  • Sales stage
  • Expected close date
  • Opportunity amount
  • Selected products
  • Price book
  • Customer stakeholders
  • Tasks and activities
  • Related Quote

Maintaining accurate Opportunity data improves pipeline visibility, forecasting, collaboration, and follow-up.

Product and Pricing Selection

The sales representative selects the price book that matches the customer type.

Standard Price Book

Used for commercial and non-government customer opportunities.

Government Price Book

Used for public-sector and government cybersecurity initiatives.

Products are then added to the Opportunity using quantities and approved sales prices.

Quote and Proposal Workflow

After the solution and pricing have been confirmed, a Salesforce Quote is created from the Opportunity.

  • Confirm the customer Account
  • Add the primary Contact
  • Select the correct price book
  • Add products and services
  • Enter quantities and sales prices
  • Verify the total proposal value
  • Set an expiration date
  • Synchronize the Quote with the Opportunity

Activities and Follow-Up

Tasks and activities support execution throughout the customer journey.

Discovery

Schedule meetings and document business and technical requirements.

Demonstration

Coordinate product demonstrations after the customer has been qualified.

Proposal Follow-Up

Track customer feedback, objections, and next steps after presenting pricing.

Decision Support

Engage decision-makers, procurement, and technical stakeholders as needed.

Reporting and Dashboards

Salesforce Reports and Dashboards convert Opportunity data into actionable sales insights.

  • Opportunity pipeline
  • Revenue by industry
  • Opportunity stage distribution
  • Expected close dates
  • Customer and account activity
  • Overall sales performance

These insights help prioritize Opportunities and identify areas requiring additional sales attention.

Renewals and Expansion

After an Opportunity is closed, the connected customer history can support future revenue.

Renewals

Prepare for contract or subscription renewal using existing Account, Contact, product, and pricing history.

User Growth

Increase subscription quantities as the customer organization expands.

Product Upgrades

Move customers from Essentials or Professional into a higher Haze subscription tier.

Additional Services

Add Premium Support or Managed Detection and Response to improve customer outcomes.

End-to-End Business Value

The CloudShield Security workflow demonstrates how Salesforce can support the complete customer and revenue lifecycle.

  • Centralized customer data
  • Structured sales stages
  • Consistent product and pricing management
  • Connected proposal creation
  • Documented stakeholder relationships
  • Actionable pipeline reporting
  • Renewal and expansion visibility
