Product Catalog

A structured overview of CloudShield Security’s SaaS subscriptions, support offerings, and managed cybersecurity services configured within Salesforce.

Catalog Overview

CloudShield Security uses Salesforce Products to maintain a centralized catalog of cybersecurity subscriptions and services.

These product records can be added to opportunities and quotes, allowing sales representatives to build customer proposals using consistent product names, pricing, and service options.

Haze Essentials

Haze Essentials is the entry-level cybersecurity subscription designed for smaller organizations or customers with straightforward security needs.

Ideal Customer

Small businesses and organizations beginning to formalize their cybersecurity programs.

Primary Value

Foundational protection delivered through an accessible SaaS subscription.

Haze Professional

Haze Professional is the primary mid-tier offering within the CloudShield product catalog. It is designed for growing organizations that require stronger cybersecurity capabilities and broader user coverage.

The standard Salesforce price book includes Haze Professional at $120 per user.

Ideal Customer

Mid-market organizations with expanding cybersecurity, compliance, and user-management requirements.

Primary Value

A balanced subscription offering combining scalability, security, and predictable per-user pricing.

Haze Enterprise

Haze Enterprise is designed for large organizations with complex security environments, multiple departments, regulatory requirements, or significant user populations.

Ideal Customer

Enterprise, healthcare, education, manufacturing, and government organizations.

Primary Value

Scalable cybersecurity protection for complex customer environments and larger deployments.

Premium Support

Premium Support is an optional add-on that provides customers with enhanced access to support resources.

It can be added to a Salesforce opportunity or quote alongside a Haze subscription when a customer requires higher service levels or more direct assistance.

Service Model

Enhanced support access, including direct assistance and priority service.

Sales Opportunity

Supports expansion, upselling, and improved customer retention.

Managed Detection and Response

Managed Detection and Response, or MDR, is an optional managed cybersecurity service that supplements the Haze software platform.

MDR provides an additional service layer for organizations that need ongoing monitoring, investigation, and security response support.

Service Type

Recurring managed cybersecurity service.

Customer Benefit

Provides additional operational support beyond the core SaaS subscription.

Salesforce Product Management

Each offering is maintained as a Salesforce Product record and can be associated with one or more price books.

Products are selected as opportunity line items and quote line items, helping maintain consistency between the customer’s needs, the sales opportunity, and the final proposal.

  • Centralized product records
  • Standardized product names
  • Opportunity line-item management
  • Quote line-item management
  • Support for add-ons and expansion
  • Consistent pricing through price books

Catalog Strategy

The product catalog was designed to support a land-and-expand SaaS sales model.

Customers can begin with a core Haze subscription and later expand through additional users, upgraded subscription tiers, Premium Support, or Managed Detection and Response.
